Dimer Lab

Draw a bipartite graph on the torus, or paste a superpotential or a Kasteleyn matrix, and read off the Goncharov–Kenyon integrable system: the toric diagram, spectral curve, Hamiltonians, Casimirs and their Poisson brackets, each checked exactly.

  • Kasteleyn matrix, perfect matchings, zig-zag paths, quiver and superpotential
  • Loops traced by hand on the dimer, with their brackets against faces, zig-zags and Hamiltonians
  • Generalized toric polygons: merge external legs of the (p, q)-web on one 7-brane and see the frozen system
  • Birational partners: every one-step mutation of the spectral curve, with the map and the matching of Hamiltonians
  • All thirty reflexive-polygon tilings and abelian orbifolds of ℂ³ built in
